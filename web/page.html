    How it's measured

    What's included

    Emails in the research.smallcase@niveshaay.com mailbox: Inbox, Sent Items and Archive. The Subscription folder (automatic smallcase notices) and the In-Place Archive (mail before 23 Jul 2026) are not included. The copy of the data is kept permanently, so it stays here after Outlook moves older mail to the archive.

    Conversations

    Emails are grouped into one conversation by subject and client address, and by smallcase ticket number (TKT-…) where there is one. Mail from vendors, cold sales and system senders is left out.

    What counts as a client query

    • An email from a client, sent to us directly.
    • An email from smallcase that hands a query to us ("Hi Team Niveshaay", "smallcase manager", "please provide an update").
    • Not counted: smallcase replying to a client itself, ticket acknowledgements, feedback surveys and auto-replies.

    Reply time

    First reply is the time from the first client message to our first email back, counted in working hours (Monday to Saturday, 09:30–18:30 IST). The target line is 4 working hours. A chaser is another client message sent before we reply.

    Status

    • Replied: our last email came after the client's last message.
    • Closed by client: the client's last message only thanks, confirms or agrees.
    • No reply on record: the client's last message has no email reply after it. It may have been handled by phone.

    Email previews

    Clicking a number, bar, chip or row lists the conversations behind it, with the client's first message, their latest message and our last reply. These are the previews Outlook keeps (about the first 250 characters of each email, without quoted earlier mail), so long emails are cut short.

    Query types

    Assigned by keywords in the subject and the first lines of the first message, in this order: refund or cancellation, renewal pricing or discount, payment or mandate, KYC, broker or account, platform issue, stock or portfolio advice, subscription or plan info, then other. Only email previews (about 250 characters) were read, so some conversations are mislabelled.
